Core Fundamentals

What Actually is a Computer?

A computer is a programmable electronic device that manipulates data according to a list of instructions (a program). It possesses the inherent ability to store, retrieve, and process massive information .

1. Well-Defined Responses Responds to specific instructions in a strictly deterministic manner .
2. Pre-Recorded Programs Executes entire sequences of pre-recorded instructions automatically .
3. Ultra-Fast Retrieval Quickly stores and retrieves large amounts of operational data .
💡 Historical Fact: The word "computer" was first recorded in 1613 by Richard Braithwait to describe people whose actual profession was calculating mathematical tables !

Hardware Evolution

The 5 Generations of Electronic Computers

From massive vacuum tube chambers to microscopic silicon wafer architecture.

1st
1946 – 1958

Vacuum Tubes

Magnetic drums for memory, machine language, room-sized footprint .
Examples: ENIAC, EDVAC, UNIVAC I & II .

2nd
1959 – 1963

Transistors

Replaced vacuum tubes; faster, smaller, symbolic assembly languages, punch cards .
Examples: UNIVAC III, RCA 501 .

3rd
1965 – 1970

Integrated Circuits

Many transistors on a single chip . Nanosecond calculations, keyboards, and monitors introduced .

4th
1971 – Present

Microprocessors

Thousands of transistors per chip; introduced graphical user interfaces (GUIs), mouse, and laptops .

5th
Today – Future

Artificial Intelligence

Parallel processing and superconductors powering AI, neural networks, and voice recognition .
